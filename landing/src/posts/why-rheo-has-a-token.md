Rheo started as an ambitious experiment in high-performance local AI. The latest neural speech models dropped, and I wanted to push the boundaries of offline speech synthesis, building a robust architecture to load voice profiles and generate speech at zero latency. The interface was crafted to feel like a sovereign recording studio — profiles as cards, a floating generation workspace, a timeline spanning the app, and precision controls.

It crossed a million downloads and gained widespread traction across the community entirely organically. Along the way it became a sovereign alternative for voice synthesis, cloning, and fast dictation.

So a fair question keeps coming up: why an ecosystem token? Why not just put it behind a subscription, or turn on sponsorships and call it a day?

## The honest version

I could have built this as a closed cloud service with recurring monthly subscriptions. I chose open source instead, and I'd make the same call again. People trust local-first tools when they can inspect the code and run it directly on their own hardware with zero external dependencies.

Maintaining and advancing deep neural audio architecture requires significant infrastructure and compute. The token directly funds ongoing engineering, GPU testing matrices, and full-time dedication to Rheo.

## What the token is — and what it isn't

**$RHEO is entirely optional.** Rheo is, and always will be, free, open source, and local-first. Every feature works without ever touching the token. It exists for supporters who want to back the project and have some fun — nothing here is gated behind it, and nothing here is financial advice.

It's also the **only** token I will ever deploy. I deployed it directly so liquidity could be transparently locked and verifiable on-chain.

## Don't trust — verify

A token only earns trust through actions you can check on-chain. So:

- Liquidity and a portion of dev holdings were **locked at launch**, visible from day one.
- I've done **buyback and burns** — buying $RHEO back from the market and burning it to a dead address, permanently. I've done this more than once, and I'll keep doing it.
- The plan is a balanced mix: lock more for trust, burn periodically, but keep enough flexibility to fund real expenses and add liquidity when it helps.

Every one of these is linked on the [token page](/token), so you never have to take my word for it. Verify the contract address there before you do anything — impersonators are common.

## What the money actually builds

Going full-time means the roadmap moves faster. The near-term priorities:

- **The mobile app.** I use the prototype every day for dictation on the go. It does both cloning and dictation, and it's nearly ready to ship.
- **Encrypted cloud backup & sync.** Generate on the go, keep your captures and generations safe, pick up on any device. This will be a paid service — around $12/year — and **free for token holders**.
- **More engines and broader hardware support.** More TTS engines, better GPU and OS coverage, so Rheo runs great on whatever you've got.

That's the whole pitch. The app stays free forever, the token is an optional way to support it and unlock the cloud service, and the proof of good faith is on-chain and in the changelog. If you want to back the project, the [token page](/token) is here — but starring the repo and telling a friend helps just as much.

Thanks for being here. Now back to shipping.
